About us

Where home-style cooking meets grand events

Hearty Caterers brings the warmth of a home kitchen to events of every size. Our cooks prepare every dish fresh on the day, our service team handles the buffet and the banana-leaf rows alike, and our décor crew sets the stage, tables and seating so the whole event comes from one trusted team.

Fresh ingredients, cooked on the day

Vegetables, meat and spices sourced each morning. Nothing is reheated from the day before.

Kerala and multi-cuisine menus

Appam and duck roast, dum biryani, North Indian, Chinese and continental, all from one kitchen.

On time, every time

Food is ready and the counters are set before your first guest walks in.

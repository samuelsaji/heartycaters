FAQs

Everything you need to know

How early should we book?

For weddings and large events, 4–8 weeks ahead is ideal, especially in the wedding season. Smaller parties can often be arranged within a week.

Do you offer customised menus?

Yes. Pick any dishes from the Custom Menu on our Menu page, or start from one of our set menus and swap dishes. We can also adjust spice levels and cater for vegetarian needs.

Is there a minimum guest count?

We cater events from around 50 guests upwards. For smaller gatherings, ask us about party trays.

Can we have a tasting before confirming?

Yes. Once you shortlist a menu, we arrange a tasting session at our kitchen for your family.

Do you handle décor and seating as well?

Yes. Stage décor, entrance, table settings and chair covers can be added to your catering package.

Are water bottles and service staff included?

Our menus include 300 ml water bottles and a uniformed service team. Counters, crockery and clean-up are part of the package.
